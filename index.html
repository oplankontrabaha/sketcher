User Guide
  • Select the region, site, type, and name of waterway. Each box can be typed into or opened as a list, and typing a waterway name searches the whole region.
  • If the waterway you are reporting about is not found in the list, use Register waterway beside the Name box to add it.
  • Click or tap near a segment to mark or unmark it.
  • When the pointer turns into an excavator, click and drag along the channel to mark a continuous stretch.
  • Existing cleaned points are loaded automatically and shown in orange.
  • Reset restores the condition that was loaded from the spreadsheet.
  • One waterway per submission. Mark the cleaned stretches of a single waterway and submit before moving on. Switching to another waterway, site, or region discards any marks or volume you have not yet submitted.
  • You may view every region, but you can only sketch and submit on waterways under the region you signed in with.
  • Volume dredged is optional and is in cubic meters (cu.m). Update to replaces the total on record with the figure you enter; Update by adds your figure to it. It is saved by the same Submit button. Percentage is the total on record as a share of the registered target volume.
  • The sticks beside Progress, in the bar above the map, follow the waterway from start to end: orange is cleaned, dark blue is not yet cleaned. A segment is the stretch between two neighbouring points.
  • Email is optional. Enter an address to receive a confirmation of the submission. Addresses used before on this device are offered as suggestions.
  • The round arrow button at the right end of the blue header folds the header down to one slim row, and opens it again. The choice is remembered on this device.
  • The magnifier button at the lower right of the map brings the open waterway, or all waterways shown, back into view.
  • Photos for the current selection appear at the bottom of this panel, all of them, newest first: scroll inside the collage to see the rest. Point at a picture to see a red pin on the map where it was taken, when the photo has a location, or click it to view it larger. Use Submit photos to add more.
